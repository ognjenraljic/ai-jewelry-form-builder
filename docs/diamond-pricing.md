# Diamond chart and watch price list (27.11.2026)

District's price lists (Enrique, Excel "District Jewelers Diamond Pricing", Nov 2026, plus watch notes) in the calculator, the Pricing tab and the portal assistant.

- **Diamond chart** (`pricing.diamonds = { on, chart: { m10: { ct, natural, lab, pave, prong, bezel } … m100 } }`, 91 rows 1.0 to 10.0 mm): retail price for ONE round stone (G-H, VS2-SI1) and setting labor per stone. When on, natural and lab diamonds in every estimate use it: stones = stones that cover the piece (`stoneLayout`) × price per stone at that size, plus pavé labor × stones (minimum per piece); center stones (ring, studs) use the row with the nearest carat plus prong labor. Chart lines are retail: the markup goes only on metal and labor. Off = the old price per carat.
- **Rules** (`pricing.diamondRules`): better grade (D-F, VVS, IF, Flawless) +20%, lower grade (I-J, SI2, I1) -15%, fancy shapes -10%, complex work +35% on labor, platinum +25% on setting labor, customer stone fee $10 per stone, minimums (pavé $75 up to 4 mm, $100 over 4 mm, prong $50, bezel $50). The estimate applies grade, platinum and minimums; fancy shapes, complex work and the customer stone fee are for hand quotes and the assistant.
- **Other setting work** (`pricing.settingExtra`): channel, invisible or tension, flush, re-tipping, stone removal (hand quotes and the assistant).
- **Watch price list** (`pricing.watch.moissanite`): stainless steel watch with moissanite, final price with stones, setting and labor, no markup: fully iced $2500, half iced $1400, face $800, bezel $500, bracelet $1200, all 3 $2500 (two parts are added up).
- **Natural or lab diamond watch**: stones from the diamond chart, setting and labor from the watch price list (same work as moissanite). The chart alone would add pavé $15 for each of ~1,600 stones of a 40 mm bust-down ($38k for lab); with the price list a 40 mm lab watch is about $15,600 fully iced and $5,400 half iced.
- **Pricing tab**: sections Metals & labor, Diamonds & stones, Watches, Grillz, Chains, Quote & alerts (all rendered, inactive ones hidden, so Save keeps every field); sticky Save bar; chart as a table with grouped headers, size filter chips, lab savings; live example tables.
- **Portal assistant**: `pricingText()` in `src/assistant.js` puts every table and rule plus example estimates from the same calculator in the prompt; `pricingLookup()` adds the exact chart row for sizes, carats, stone counts, grade and platinum named in the question.
- Migration `0043_district_diamond_chart.sql` writes the chart, rules, other work and watch list for District (only fields it does not have yet). Checks: `scripts/diamond-pricing-check.mjs` (calculator), `scripts/pricing-tab-check.mjs` (save and load).

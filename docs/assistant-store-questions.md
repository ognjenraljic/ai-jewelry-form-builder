# Customer page assistant: what we need from the store

The assistant on each customer page already knows everything on that page: the customer's design and choices, their budget, the prices of Option 1 and Option 2, the price hold countdown, the deposit and booking buttons, open times, booked appointments and the store info from the Pricing tab. It answers only from that and from what the store writes in **Portal → Bookings → Assistant on customer pages**. Anything else goes to the team (Notifications + the chat on the lead).

The more of this the store answers, the fewer questions end up with the team.

## 1. Time

- How long does each piece take? Grillz (after the mold), chains, pendants, rings, bracelets, earrings, watch bust-downs.
- Rush orders: possible? Extra cost?
- When does the clock start: deposit, mold, or design approval?

## 2. Deposit and payment

- How much is the deposit (amount or percent)? Does it depend on the piece?
- When is the rest due (pickup, before shipping, in parts)?
- Payment methods (card, cash, Zelle, Cash App, crypto)?
- Financing or payment plans (Affirm, Klarna, layaway)? If not, say so.
- Is the deposit refundable? Until when?

## 3. Molds, pickup and shipping

- In-studio mold: how long does it take, appointment or walk-in?
- Mold kit by mail: cost, how it works, what if the mold is bad?
- Shipping: which states or countries, insured, tracking, cost, signature?
- Local pickup: when and where, ID needed?

## 4. Warranty and care

- What is covered, and for how long? Lost stones? Broken clasps?
- Free cleaning, polishing, re-dipping?
- Repairs and resizing: possible, cost?
- Grillz: removable or permanent, eating and drinking, cleaning. (Health questions always go to the team and a dentist.)

## 5. Design changes

- How many free changes before production? Is there a CAD or sketch approval step?
- Can the customer change the design after paying the deposit?

## 6. Metals and stones

- Karats offered (10K, 14K, 18K, 925 silver), solid gold vs plated.
- Lab diamonds vs natural vs moissanite, in plain words.
- Clarity (VVS, VS), certificates (GIA, IGI), stone sizes.
- Dental gold: what it is and who it is for.

## 7. Price

- Can customers negotiate, or is the quote final? Price match?
- Is there a cash discount or any standing promotion?
- What happens after the price hold expires?

## 8. The store

- Address, hours, parking, appointment or walk-in.
- Languages spoken (the assistant replies in the customer's language, for example Spanish).
- What the store is known for, years in business, clients the store allows us to mention.
- Instagram, phone or text number for a real person, and how fast the team usually replies.

## 9. Real questions

- The 10 to 20 questions customers ask most in DMs, texts and calls, with the store's usual answer. Copying real conversations (without names) is the fastest way.
- Anything the assistant must never say or promise.

# Price calculator: questions for Enrique (District Jewelers Denver)

The portal now has a **Pricing** tab and two boxes next to every lead: an estimated price for what the customer picked, and a "fits the budget" version (for example moissanite instead of diamonds, 14K instead of 18K, dental gold). The numbers come only from prices you enter. To make the formula match how you quote, please answer:

1. **Gold price**: do you quote from the daily gold spot price? Should the portal pull it automatically every day (free public source, about $124 per gram of pure gold today), or do you enter it yourself?
2. **Karats**: is the price of 10K / 14K / 18K simply gold price × purity (41.7% / 58.3% / 75%), or do you use your own price per gram for each karat?
3. **Other metals**: your price per gram for dental gold, white gold, rose gold, two-tone, platinum and silver (or how you calculate them).
4. **Weight**: about how many grams of metal per tooth cap? Is it different for front teeth and premolars, open face, or deep cut? For other pieces (pendant, ring, chain per inch, bracelet), typical grams?
5. **Labor**: your labor or making charge per tooth (and per piece for other jewelry). Is there a minimum per order, or a mold/impression fee?
6. **Stones**: price per tooth for each stone type (moissanite, lab-grown, natural) at each coverage level (accents, half iced, fully iced). Does the setting (pave, straight, invisible, jumbo, mosaic) or clarity (SI, VS, VVS, Flawless) change the price, and by how much?
7. **Cuts**: extra charge per tooth for each cut (diamond cut, weed cut, starburst...)?
8. **Markup**: one markup percentage on top of everything, or different by piece or material?
9. **Budget fit**: when a customer is over budget, which swaps do you prefer to suggest first (cheaper stones, lower karat, less ice, dental gold, fewer teeth)? Any swap you never suggest?
10. **Display**: should the estimate show as one number, or as a range (for example ±10%)?

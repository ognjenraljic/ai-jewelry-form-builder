FAQ

Good questions.

Is the render exactly what I will make?

No, and the widget says so. It's a concept preview that shows the customer the style, metal, stones and text they chose. It gets them excited and gives you a clear starting point. Your final piece is still handcrafted to your standards.

How accurate are the renders?

Very close on what matters: piece type, shape, metal color, stone coverage and spelled text. Styles are guided by reference photos, and when those are photos of your own pieces, renders look even more like your work.

Do I need a developer?

No. The widget is two lines of code that you paste on any page, and we can install it for you during setup. It works on Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and custom sites.

How long does a render take?

About 10 to 20 seconds for the first render and under 10 seconds for refinements.

Can people spam it or run up costs?

Renders only start after a customer leaves their contact details and consents to be contacted. Every lead has a render limit, and there are rate limits per visitor. Phone verification is available on Growth and Pro.

Who owns the leads and renders?

You do. Leads go to your inbox, your dashboard or your CRM, and you can use the renders in your follow-ups.

Can I use my own pieces as styles?

Yes, and we recommend it. Send us photos of your signature pieces and we turn them into styles your customers can pick from.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. The setup fee covers configuring your styles, branding and installation.
